dejavu-sub.ttf and dejavu-sub.otf are a tiny subset of DejaVu Sans (Bitstream Vera / DejaVu fonts license), only the glyphs for "goome! HiAVWTy\u00e9.@".
The images are the same test files c-goome uses (made by its tests/mkimages.py).
colr.ttf, cbdt.ttf and sbix.ttf are tiny made up test fonts from c-goome (made by its tests/mkfonts.py). colr.ttf has COLR/CPAL emoji, hebrew, arabic forms, a ccmp ligature and a cmap 14 heart, cbdt.ttf and sbix.ttf hold one png cat.
